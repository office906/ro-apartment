Once an architecture studio

For a time, this flat was the studio of HAA&D, Hagar Abiri Architecture & Design. Today it is the guest apartment of the practice, kept with the same care for light, proportion and materials.

Some of the studio's drawings still hang on the walls.

About HAA&D

The flat

A separate bedroom, a full kitchen and a living room whose tall north-east windows look straight into the courtyard garden, which is rare in this part of Berlin. The light stays soft and even through the day.

It was renovated and is looked after by its architect-owner: original wooden floors, new windows, quality furniture and designer lighting. The large table works as a proper home office.

You meet Hagar, the owner, in person at move-in. She lives in Berlin and is reachable during your stay.

A green courtyard behind the house

The flat faces the garden, not the street. Trees, roses climbing the balconies and a small seating corner: the quiet side of a lively neighbourhood.

Who it's for

People moving to Berlin for work, international students, interns and professionals on a temporary assignment. One person, minimum two months, maximum six.

This is temporary furnished housing (Wohnen auf Zeit), not a holiday rental. Tourist stays are not accepted. A written rental agreement is signed before move-in.

Questions

What is the minimum and maximum stay?

Minimum 2 months, maximum 6 months.

Can I register my address (Anmeldung)?

Yes. You get the landlord confirmation (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung) you need for your appointment at the Bürgeramt.

How much is the rent, and what does it include?

The monthly rent is given on request, as it depends on the season and length of stay. It includes all bills: heating, electricity, water and Wi-Fi.

Is there a deposit?

Yes, one month's rent, paid within 3 days of the booking. Your dates are confirmed once the deposit has arrived.

Can two people stay?

No, the apartment is rented to one person.

Can I book it for a holiday?

No. It's temporary housing for people living, working or studying in Berlin.

Can I view it before signing?

If you're already in Berlin, yes. If not, we can do a video call walkthrough.

How does booking work?

Send a request with your dates. I reply within 24 hours with the rent for your stay, we have a short call, and I send the rental agreement. The booking is confirmed once the deposit of one month's rent has arrived, within 3 days. Without it, the dates are released.
